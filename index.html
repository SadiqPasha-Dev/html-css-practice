Chocolate Cake with Honey

recipe of delicious Chocolate cake with honey

Ingredients List

  • Raw Chocolate
  • Raw Honey
  • Wheat flour and eggs
  • Salt
  • Cream

Instructions

  1. Mix Wheat flour and eggs with some water until fluffy
  2. Pour honey on top with a pinch of salt
  3. Mix all Ingredients, rest it for 2 hours
  4. Mix cream and choclate, & bake it until fluffy and decorate it.

Preparation Time

Prep Cook Total
10 minutes with 2 hour resting 25 minutes at 30 degree Celsius Cook time 155 minutes
